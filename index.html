How item rules work

Every safety item has its own editable rule. Choose the rule and limit for each item in the table above, then press Save on that row. “Not set” blocks issuing until you decide the policy.

For damage/replacement and supervisor approval rules, record a factual reason and confirm the checkbox on the issue form. Changes apply to future issue checks; existing history is retained.
